# Privacy Policy — Coinflow

Effective 26 August 2026.

Coinflow is a crypto price widget published by **ElecTream** (Leeam Sweeney).

## What we collect

- **On-device settings.** Followed coins, widget tabs, themes, and alert thresholds stay on the phone (DataStore / Room).
- **Wallet addresses you type.** Optional. Used only to query public chain APIs for a balance. Addresses may be included in Android backups (`allowBackup` is currently on).
- **Market data.** Prices and sparklines are fetched from Kraken and, for wallets, from public RPC / explorer endpoints (Blockstream, Ethereum RPC, Solana RPC, XRPL). Those services see the IP of your device and any address you submitted.

There is no ElecTream account. We do not sell this data. We do not use it for ads.

## Why we need it

Prices, the widget, alerts, and optional balances are the product.

## Retention and deletion

Clear coins, wallets, and alerts in the app, or uninstall. Uninstalling removes local DataStore/Room data.

## Permissions (Android)

Internet, notifications (alerts), boot completed (reschedule widget/work), exact alarms on older APIs, foreground service declared (unused typed service).
